# Tracker Layout

**File:** [tracker.csv](tracker.csv). In Google Sheets, go to File → Import → Upload, and the formulas will work. Format the rate cells as %.

Fill in one row per shift before you clock out. It takes 5 minutes.

## Pace (filled in automatically)

- **S, Joins to date:** running total.
- **T, Pace target:** 60 ÷ 13 shifts × shifts worked. That's about 4.6 per shift.
- **U, Ahead/behind:** positive means you're ahead.

## Rates block (below the totals)

These compare your real numbers to the planning assumptions: 25% contact, 30% booking, 60% show, and 50% close.
**On Dec 6,** replace the assumptions with your real numbers and redo the math:

> Outreach needed per shift = joins needed ÷ (contact rate × booking rate × show rate × close rate), adjusted for walk-ins.

**Example:** if your close rate is really 35%, not 50%, you need about 13 tours per shift instead of 9 to hit 60. That's not possible on one desk. So the answer is better tours, not more outreach.

## Pace math, honestly

- November has only **9 of your shifts left** (Nov 13-29). If units count by calendar month, 4.6 per shift gets you about **41 in November**, not 60. Ask your GM how your first month is measured.
- The 13th row (12/11) is there so you can see a full 13-shift cycle.
