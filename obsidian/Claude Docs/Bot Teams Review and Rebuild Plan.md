# Bot Teams Review and Rebuild Plan

Review of the bot teams built with Grok. Nothing here is set up yet. It all waits on your go.

## The short version

About 30 bots across 9 teams. Most output is drafts and plans, and the two teams that ran the most (clipping and trading) made the least. Call: **keep 4 teams, merge 1, hold 1, cut 3.** That leaves 8 bots, about 4 scheduled runs a week instead of roughly 110 a day.

> Numbers from Grok's summary are Grok's record, not independently checked.

## Calls by team

| Team | Call | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| RichyRich.TV content | GO, slimmed | Main brand. Real output exists, but 9 bots for 1 public Short is too many. |
| Cooked On Stream clipping | KILL | 109 clips, 61 Instagram posts, 2 followers, $0 paid. New accounts don't get the views campaigns pay for. |
| Ifá services and content | GO | A real service people pay for, 8 TikToks live, and Ifá posts beat the main-account average. |

## New names: 8 bots

1. **Content Planner** (was Rey, Zoe, Andy and Dana's ideas): weekly plan, hooks, captions, titles, trend picks, plus Ifá and fitness post ideas.
2. **Video Editor** (was Jim, Dana and Aiden Jr): turns streams and raw footage into 3–5 shorts and an 8–12 minute cut.
3. **Bookings and Outreach** (was Joey and Ashley): finds podcast guests, shows to appear on and speaking spots. Drafts pitches only.
4. **Designer** (was Gio): logos, thumbnails, banners, flyers, on request.
5. **Sunday Scorecard** (was Frank): one weekly numbers review across accounts.
6. **Fitness Coach** (was Lexi and Marco): weekly check-in, training notes and grocery list.
7. **Ifá Study Helper** (was Akọ̀wé): study and reference, cites sources, never invents verses. The Ọ̀sẹ̀ reminder becomes a plain reminder, not a bot.
8. **Credit Dispute Drafter** (was Carla): one-time drafts you file yourself.

Cut or not carried over: Nia (inbox), Ava (avatar — folds into the clone platform project), Scotty, Theo, Dre, Mike, Vince, Jeffrey, Donnie, Sal, the 8 product-loop roles, and the setup bot. No CEO bot or setup bot needed. Tony, Maya and Derek stay parked with Home Quotes. The private support coach isn't part of this rebuild; it stays personal.

## Light schedule

- **Monday 9 AM:** Content Planner sends the week's plan.
- **Wednesday 10 AM:** Bookings and Outreach sends new leads and draft pitches.
- **Sunday 6 PM:** Fitness check-in.
- **Sunday 7 PM:** Sunday Scorecard.
- **Every 4th day:** Ọ̀sẹ̀ reminder (a reminder, not a bot run).
- **On request:** Video Editor, Designer, Ifá Study Helper, Credit Dispute Drafter.

Nothing runs every 15 minutes. Nothing is always on.

## Hard limits

- No placing, changing or cancelling trades or bets on any market (Kalshi, Polymarket, Public), even with a yes or API key. Research and a journal of your own trades is the max.
- No holding passwords or API keys.
- Every post, clip submission, outreach message and ad spend needs a yes on that item.
- No moving money at banks or credit services. Credit disputes are drafts you file yourself.
