# RichyRich.TV Podcast Format

## Show name

Pick: **The Real Deal with Richy Rich** — plays off the Real Deal Richy name and says what the show is: talking straight and joking about everything.

- **The Real Deal with Richy Rich:** the pick, easy to say and search.
- **Rich Off Topic:** fits a show that goes anywhere, but sounds less like a brand.

**Pitch line:** a former FSU lineman from Tampa roasts the news, breaks down sports and life, and lets chat pick the rest.

## The 4 segments

Same four segments, same order, every episode.

| Segment | What it is | Why it works | Length |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Pancake Report** | 3 headlines from the week: one line on what happened, then flatten it with jokes and your take. | News gives fresh, searchable topics every week. The roast makes it yours. | 12–15 min |
| **Film Room** | Break down a game, viral clip or athlete's story like a coach breaking film. | A D1 lineman's eye is something most podcasters can't fake; sports pulls in men 28–38. | 10–12 min |
| **The Second Half** | Life after football: starting over, training, money moves, psych classes, Cuba trips. | The personal part is why people subscribe to *you*. Also feeds The Comeback. | 10 min |
| **You Pick It** | Chat picks the next topic off the topic card, or answer comments and DMs. | Rewards showing up live; comes back every week. | 8–10 min |

**Pancake Report rule:** check the story at a real news source before roasting it, and joke about the powerful and the ridiculous, not victims. Keeps the channel ad-safe.

## Episode 1 rundown (~57 min, solo)

The Second Half carries the origin story this week, since new viewers need to know who you are.

| Time | Part | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–1:00 | Cold open | Live: read tonight's topic card. YouTube edit: open on the funniest 30 seconds. |
| 1:00–4:00 | Intro | Who you are in 60 seconds (Tampa, FSU, what you do now), show name and the 4 segments. |
| 4:00–18:00 | The Pancake Report | 3 headlines, ~4 min each: what happened, then the roast. |
| 18:00–19:00 | Quick plug | Follow on TikTok and IG @richyrich.tv, subscribe on YouTube @RichyRich_TV. |
| 19:00–31:00 | Film Room | One game, clip or athlete story from the week, broken down like film. |
| 31:00–43:00 | The Second Half | How football ended at FSU and what came after. Keep it funny where you can. |
| 43:00–54:00 | You Pick It | Chat picks 2–3 topics off the card. If chat is quiet, use backup picks. |
| 54:00–57:00 | Outro | One takeaway line, tease next week's Second Half topic, plug socials again. |

**Prep before going live:**
- [ ] Pick the 3 headlines and the Film Room clip
- [ ] Write 3 bullet points per segment
- [ ] Fill the topic card with 6 backup topics

## Stream and shorts

One live stream on Kick makes the episode, the YouTube video and a week of shorts.

### As a Kick stream
- Tuesday's stream = the podcast; Saturday stays IRL. Lines up with Wednesday shorts day and Friday YouTube edit.
- Put the 4 segment names on screen as the topic card; check each off so late joiners know where you are.
- Record a local copy while streaming so the YouTube edit isn't stuck with stream quality.
- Read chat out loud during You Pick It only — keeps the other three tight.

### On YouTube
- Cut dead air and the plug break, open on the best 30 seconds, post Friday as the full episode.
- Title it after the biggest headline or best story, not "Episode 1".

### Cut into shorts (~7 per episode, one a day)
1. One per Pancake Report headline: the best roast line (3 shorts).
2. The key moment from Film Room.
3. One story moment from The Second Half.
4. The funniest chat moment from You Pick It.
5. One spare: whatever got the biggest reaction live.

Keep each short 20–45 seconds, start on the punchline or hook, add captions, crop vertical. Clipping tools: see [[Free Tool Stack]].
