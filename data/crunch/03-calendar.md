# 4-Week Calendar: Nov 13 - Dec 6, 2026

Your shifts: **Fri 12-8, Sat 10-7, Sun 10-7.** That's 12 shifts in this window.

Every shift, no matter the theme: the daily floor in [README.md](README.md).
Campaigns: (1) POS trifecta, (2) guest pass routes, (3) B2B, (4) web lead cadence, (5) Start Before the Rush, (6) Crunch en Español.

**Rule of thumb:** Friday = outreach and B2B. Saturday and Sunday = floor, tours, VIP lists.
