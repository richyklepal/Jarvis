## Pushback: what isn't realistic

**The workload**
- **One shift can't hold 100 outreach, 9 tours, 25 passes, and front desk coverage.** A 9-tour day alone is about 6 hours of tours and closing. On busy weekends, outreach will be 30-60 attempts at most. Put the heavy outreach on Fridays.
- **The four rates multiply.** If any one of them comes in low (and in month one they usually do), you'd need 150+ outreach attempts per shift. That doesn't fit. Better tours (closing rate) fix the math faster than more dialing.
- **"300 outreach a day" and "125 passes a day" read like club totals,** not one rep's. Confirm with your GM.

**The target**
- **60 units in November isn't realistic.** You have 9 shifts left this month, so pace math says about 41. Plus you're still learning the CRM. A good month-one goal is to hit the *process* floor every shift and track real rates. Push for 60 in December.
- **3-5 VIP names per member is the goal, but 2 is the real-world floor.** And only names the member actually texts will turn into visits.

**Campaigns**
- **Six campaigns is too many for 3 shifts a week.** Your core is #1 (POS trifecta), #2 (passes), and #4 (web leads). Treat #3 (B2B), #5 (pre-sale), and #6 (Spanish) as layers on top of those. #6 isn't really separate: it's how you do the other five.
- **B2B on weekends is weak.** HR and managers work weekdays. Use the Friday 12-4 block. On weekends, only target places open then: dealerships, Publix, Walmart.
- **Steinbrenner Field, Raymond James, the hospitals, and HCC** have corporate wellness departments and formal vendor processes. One walk-in won't land them. Ask your GM if CR Fitness already has a relationship there before you spend time on them.
- **H Mart isn't open yet.** Park it.
- **Start Before the Rush** depends on the 1¢ promo still running. If FALL ends in November, the December pitch needs whatever promo corporate approves next.
- **The guest-pass walk route** should be 10-15 minutes, max. You're needed at the desk.

**Compliance traps in the guide itself**
- The guide's "5-star Google review" fallback for duplication **conflicts with Google policy and the FTC rule.** Use the honest review ask in the scripts, separate from the shirt.
- **Facebook shares as a daily count:** 10 shares only happen if you're closing about 5 members a day. They're an output, not something you can grind.

## Compliance guardrails (short version)

- Texts and autodialed calls only to people with written consent, only from club tools. Never from your personal phone.
- Calls 8 AM-8 PM. Max 3 calls to one person in 24 hours. "Stop" means stop, right away. Check Do Not Call.
- No AI voice calls to leads.
- Emails: honest subject, club address, unsubscribe link.
- Reviews: never ask for "5 stars," never reward a review. The T-shirt is for the Facebook steps only.
- Referrals: the member texts their own friends. You don't cold-text those numbers.
- Only approved Crunch creative and promos. Get approval before posting, sending, or buying anything.
