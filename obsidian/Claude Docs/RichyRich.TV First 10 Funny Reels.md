# RichyRich.TV: First 10 Funny Reels

Ten cheap, fast reels to shoot solo on a phone. One a day covers the daily floor for almost two weeks. **EASY THIS WEEK** = no location, no friend, almost no editing.

## Easiest 3 to film this week

- [ ] **#1 The "light" lineman meal** — kitchen, solo, 20 sec
- [ ] **#3 Title team, zero snaps** — talk to camera in the car, 30–45 sec
- [ ] **#7 The Buick thinks it's a Lambo** — your car, slow-mo and music, 15 sec

## The 10 reels

### 1. The "light" lineman meal (EASY THIS WEEK)
- **Format:** POV skit
- **Hook (first 2 sec):** On-screen text: "Doctor: eat light today. Me, a former 305-pound lineman:"
- **Beat:** Calmly pull out one meal-prep container, then a bigger one, then a mixing bowl, then a cooler. Straight face. End adding a side salad and nodding like that makes it light.
- **Shot:** Kitchen counter, phone on a stand, solo. Props: containers, a bowl, a small cooler.
- **Length:** 20 sec

### 2. Every gym has these 5 guys
- **Format:** Character skit (you play all five)
- **Hook:** "Every gym has these five guys. Which one are you?"
- **Beat:** Quick cuts: curls in the squat rack, 40-minute phone call on the bench, the grunter, mirror flexer between every set, the guy "warming up" for an hour. Change a hat or shirt for each.
- **Shot:** Home or garage works. Hats, towel, headphones. At a gym: check filming rules, keep strangers out of frame.
- **Length:** 35–40 sec

### 3. I was on a national title team and never played a snap (EASY THIS WEEK)
- **Format:** Ex-athlete story, talk to camera
- **Hook:** "I was on the 2013 national championship team and never played a single snap."
- **Beat:** Funniest true moments as a four-star early enrollee at FSU: first-practice reality check, eating like a king at the training table, the guy in the team photo nobody remembers. Light and self-roasting. Close: "That's why I talk so much now. I got four years of snaps to make up for."
- **Shot:** Driver's seat of the parked car, phone on the dash. Optional: old FSU photo on screen.
- **Length:** 30–45 sec

### 4. Today's headline, read by my old O-line coach
- **Format:** World-news reaction
- **Hook:** "Today's news, but my old O-line coach is reading it."
- **Beat:** One light, weird headline. Read it like a screaming pregame speech, slamming the table, ending "NOW GET ON THE BUS!" Avoid tragedies; go odd, funny or petty.
- **Shot:** Solo, in front of a wall. TikTok/CapCut green-screen with the headline behind you. Prop: whistle or clipboard.
- **Length:** 30 sec

### 5. Being the big guy means you're everybody's moving company
- **Format:** POV skit
- **Hook:** "Being the big homie means your phone looks like this every weekend."
- **Beat:** Phone lights up with fake texts: "you got a truck?", "you free Saturday?", "bro it's just one couch", "it's a 4th floor walk-up". Cut to you staring at camera, then phone face down.
- **Shot:** Couch, solo. Fake texts via a second app or screen-recorded text generator.
- **Length:** 20 sec

### 6. Street interview: "What's the most overrated thing in Tampa?"
- **Format:** IRL street bit
- **Hook:** On-screen question plus the first stranger answer, no intro.
- **Beat:** Ask 4–6 people one fun question. Keep only the funniest answers and reactions. Practice for street interviews on the road to the podcast.
- **Shot:** Riverwalk, Ybor or a busy plaza. Phone + clip-on mic. Ask before filming; only post people who say yes. Laugh with them, never at them.
- **Length:** 30–45 sec

### 7. My 2014 Buick thinks it's a Lambo (EASY THIS WEEK)
- **Format:** Car humor
- **Hook:** "Pulling up to the gym in my 2014 Buick Verano like..."
- **Beat:** Slow-mo walk-up, sunglasses, dramatic hype music, pat the hood like a supercar. Last shot: tight on the cupholder or oil dipstick with text "she's got character."
- **Shot:** Car in a parking lot, solo. Phone on the ground for low angle, slow-mo.
- **Length:** 15 sec

### 8. Rating gym exercises like Madden ratings
- **Format:** Fitness + gaming
- **Hook:** "Rating gym exercises like they're Madden players."
- **Beat:** 5 exercises, each with a player-card overlay: name, overall, one stat line. E.g. "Burpees: 62 OVR. Speed 90, Morale 12." "Leg press: 85 OVR. Ego boost 99." End on the one you'd draft first.
- **Shot:** Home or garage, solo. Overlays in CapCut, neon green and gold to match the brand.
- **Length:** 30–40 sec

### 9. Things I pack for Cuba after five trips
- **Format:** Travel bit
- **Hook:** "I've been to Cuba five times. Here's what's actually in my bag."
- **Beat:** Pull items out one at a time with a quick joke each, from real experience. Travel life, not politics; never make locals the joke.
- **Shot:** Suitcase on the bed, overhead or straight-on.
- **Length:** 30 sec

### 10. The reference photo vs. the haircut you can actually get
- **Format:** Barber skit (you play client and barber)
- **Hook:** "When the client shows you THIS reference photo..."
- **Beat:** Client holds up a phone with an impossible cut. Cut to barber slowly zooming in on face: "Bro, that's a wig in the picture." End: "We'll get you close."
- **Shot:** Bathroom mirror or a chair. Props: cape/towel, clippers, phone.
- **Length:** 20 sec

## Filming tips for all 10

- Hook as on-screen text in the first frame — many people watch muted.
- Film vertical, near a window for light, cut every pause.
- Post the same reel to TikTok, IG Reels and YouTube Shorts.
- Neon lime green and gold for all text overlays so everything reads as RichyRich.TV.
