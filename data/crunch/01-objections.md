# Objection Handlers (EN + ES)

**The pattern for all four:** Agree → Ask → Answer → Ask again.
Keep it calm. No Judgments means no pressure tactics and no bashing other gyms.

Fill in the blanks before you use these: `[Peak $__]`, `[annual fee $__]`, promo end date.

Spanish tip: start with **usted** for older guests or on the phone. Switch to **tú** when they do.

---

## 1. Price ("It's too expensive" / "Está muy caro")

**EN**
> "Totally fair. Nobody wants to pay for something they won't use.
> Is it the monthly price, or what's due today?
> *(If today)* Today you start for 1 cent, with your first week free.
> *(If monthly)* Base is $15.99. Peak Results is [Peak $__], and that includes the HIIT Zone, hot studio, and Relax & Recover. Just the hot classes run $150 or more somewhere else.
> If Peak feels tight, start on Base. Upgrading later is free.
> Which one fits your budget better?"

**ES**
> "Le entiendo. Nadie quiere pagar por algo que no va a usar.
> ¿Es la mensualidad, o lo que se paga hoy?
> *(Si es hoy)* Hoy empieza por 1 centavo, y la primera semana es gratis.
> *(Si es mensual)* Base cuesta $15.99. Peak Results es [Peak $__] e incluye la zona HIIT, el estudio caliente y Relax & Recover. Solo las clases calientes cuestan $150 o más en otro lugar.
> Si Peak le queda apretado, empiece con Base. Subir de plan después es gratis.
> ¿Cuál le queda mejor a su presupuesto?"

**Be honest:** If they ask about going down later, tell them: 90-day minimum and a $20 fee to drop to Base. That's why "start on Base, upgrade free" is the honest move for a tight budget.

## 2. "I need to think about it" ("Lo voy a pensar")

**EN**
> "Of course, it's a real decision.
> Usually when someone wants to think, it's one of three things: the price, the schedule, or whether they'll actually stick with it. Which one is it for you?"
>
> *(Handle that one thing.)*
>
> "Here's what makes it easy: it's month to month, and today it's 1 cent to start. You're not locking into a long contract. Let's get you started and book your first session with a coach. Today or tomorrow?"

**ES**
> "Claro, es una decisión importante.
> Normalmente, cuando alguien lo quiere pensar, es por una de tres cosas: el precio, el horario, o si de verdad va a ser constante. ¿Cuál es en su caso?"
>
> *(Resuelva esa una cosa.)*
>
> "Lo bueno es que es mes a mes, y hoy empieza por 1 centavo. No se amarra a un contrato largo. Vamos a empezar y le reservo su primera sesión con un entrenador. ¿Hoy o mañana?"

**If they still want to wait:** Don't push. Get their consent to follow up, book a specific time to talk, and give them a guest pass for this week. That keeps them in your pipeline.

## 3. "I need to talk to my spouse" ("Tengo que hablar con mi esposo/a")

**EN**
> "Makes total sense. You two decide things together.
> Would they want to work out too?
> *(If yes)* Perfect. Bring them in free this week and you can train together. Or if you want, give them a quick call right now, and I'll answer any questions they have.
> *(If no)* What do you think their main question would be? Let's make sure you have the answer."
>
> "If they're good with it, are you ready to start?"

**ES**
> "Tiene sentido. Ustedes deciden juntos.
> ¿A su pareja también le gustaría entrenar?
> *(Si dice que sí)* Perfecto. Tráigala gratis esta semana y entrenan juntos. O si quiere, llámela ahora mismo y yo le contesto cualquier pregunta.
> *(Si dice que no)* ¿Qué cree que le va a preguntar? Así se lleva la respuesta."
>
> "Si a su pareja le parece bien, ¿está listo/a para empezar?"

**Note:** A first-time local guest is free. Get the spouse's name for the appointment, but let the member reach out to them, not you.

---

## 4. "I already have a gym" ("Ya tengo gimnasio")

**EN**
> "Love that, you're already training. Where do you go?
> What do you like about it? And if you could change one thing, what would it be?"
>
> *(Listen. Then match one or two things Crunch does better for them.)*
>
> "That's exactly why people add Crunch: hot studio, the HIIT Zone, Relax & Recover with hydro and cryo, all in one spot on Hillsborough. A lot of members keep their other gym for a month and see which one they actually use.
> Since it's 1 cent to start, want to try it that way?"

**ES**
> "¡Qué bueno, ya está entrenando! ¿A dónde va?
> ¿Qué es lo que más le gusta? Y si pudiera cambiar una cosa, ¿qué sería?"
>
> *(Escuche. Luego conecte una o dos cosas que Crunch hace mejor para esa persona.)*
>
> "Por eso mucha gente se une a Crunch: estudio caliente, zona HIIT, Relax & Recover con hidro y crio, todo en un solo lugar en la Hillsborough. Muchos socios se quedan con su otro gimnasio un mes y ven cuál usan más.
> Como empieza por 1 centavo, ¿quiere probarlo así?"

**Don't** say anything negative about the other gym. Their answer to "what would you change?" does the selling for you.
