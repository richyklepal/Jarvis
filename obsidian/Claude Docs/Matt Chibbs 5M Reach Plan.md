# Matt Chibbs: 5M Reach Plan (90 to 120 days)

## The pick

**Mid plan: ~$25,000 over 16 weeks (~$6,000/month).** Reaches ~4M unique people from spend alone. Hitting 5M needs one clip to take off on its own — a fair bet at that volume, not a sure thing.

Split: clipping networks **$11K** (cheapest views, most reach), creator posts **$5K** (real fans using the song), paid ads **$9K** (only on clips that already proved themselves).

Straight talk: at $5K total, expect ~1M people, not 5M. Guaranteeing 5M with ads alone costs $50K–$90K.

## What "5M people" means

5M unique people ≠ 5M views. People see a song 2–3 times before it sticks, so the target is **12–15M views/impressions** across platforms.

Assumptions:
- **Paid ads:** ~2 views per person (2.5 in aggressive) → unique reach = impressions ÷ 2.
- **Clips and creator posts:** ~2.5 clips per person → unique reach = views ÷ 2.5.
- **Overlap:** ads + clips hit some of the same people → each total drops 10–15%.

Platforms report unique reach for ads directly; clip reach is an estimate.

## What each channel costs

Clipping is the cheapest reach by far; ads are 2–4× pricier per view but controllable. Published 2026 benchmarks from vendor/agency blogs, not audited rate cards; no music-only ad figures.

| Instagram Reels ads (Meta) | $4–$8 CPM Reels; entertainment/media ≈ $9.27 across Meta | Cheapest IG placement; also feeds FB Reels | [Sovran](https://sovran.ai/benchmarks/meta-ads-cpm-by-industry), [Top Growth Marketing](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/instagram-ads-cost/) |
| YouTube Shorts ads | ~$4 CPM; in-stream $5–$10 CPM, $0.02–$0.10/view | Shorts view bills only after 10 s | [Top Growth Marketing](https://topgrowthmarketing.com/youtube-ads-cost/) |
| Clipping networks | $1–$6 per 1,000 views + 3–19% fee; some music offers < $0.20 | Hundreds of fan-style accounts posting clips | [FindClout](https://findclout.com/blog/best-music-clipping) |

Plan uses **$4 per 1,000 impressions** for ads and **$1.50 per 1,000 views** for clipping. (One vendor's best case: 15.2M clip views for $1,000 — don't plan on that.)

Spotify ads build streams, not reach, and need ~1,000+ streams in the last 28 days to unlock (sources disagree). Use after the song is moving.

## Three budget tiers

| Tier (16 weeks) | Clipping | Creator posts | Paid ads | Est. unique reach | Hits 5M? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Lean: $5,000 | $2,500 (1.7M views) | $1,000 (4 posts) | $1,500 (375K impr.) | ~0.9M | Only with a viral clip |
| **Mid: $25,000** | $11,000 (7.3M views) | $5,000 (~20 posts) | $9,000 (2.25M impr.) | ~3.8M | Likely, with one clip that takes off |
| Aggressive: $60,000 | $18,000 (12M views) | $10,000 (~40 posts) | $32,000 (8M impr.) | ~7M | Yes, with room to spare |
| Ads only (comparison) | — | — | $50,000–$87,500 | 5M | Yes, but no organic lift and fewer real fans |

Reach math: clip views ÷ 2.5 + creator views (~30K/post) ÷ 2.5 + ad impressions ÷ 2 (÷ 2.5 aggressive) − 10–15% overlap.

Why mixing beats ads alone: clipping costs ~⅓ of ads per view, and creator posts trigger free organic videos with the sound — that's what usually closes 4M → 5M. Cheaper-country targeting can halve ad cost, but those listeners stream less.

## Organic and creator tactics (in order of payoff)

1. **Clipping campaigns:** pay per 1,000 views to clippers posting the song's best 7–15 seconds across hundreds of accounts. Pay on verified views only, cap the rate. [FindClout](https://findclout.com/blog/best-music-clipping) lists marketplaces at $1–$6 per 1,000.
2. **Sound seeding:** pay 15–20 small creators (10K–100K followers) to post with the sound in week one of a push.
3. **One clear hook or trend:** one simple thing to copy per song (caption format, transition, short dance).
4. **Spark the winners:** ads only on posts that already beat average watch time. Spark keeps likes/follows on the original.
5. **Matt's engineer angle:** studio breakdowns, before/after mixes, beat builds — content most artists can't make.
6. **Playlist pitching:** pitch in Spotify for Artists 7+ days out, and to independent curators once there's traction.

## Week-by-week plan (mid tier, 16 weeks)

Test cheap first, then put most money behind the song + hook that proves itself by week 4. Weeks count from the day the first clips go live.

| Weeks | Phase | Spend | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1–2 | Set up | $1,000 | Pick 2–3 finished songs. Cut 30+ clips per song. Set up TikTok/IG/YouTube business accounts, Spotify for Artists, one smart link. Post daily for a baseline. |
| 3–4 | Test | $3,000 | Ads at $20–$50/day on 3–5 clips per platform. 5 creator posts. Small clipping test ($1,000) per song. Find the best watch-time song + hook. |
| 5–8 | Scale the winner | $9,000 | Clipping budget on the winner. 10 more creator posts in one week. Ads to $80–$100/day, winners only. Spotify Showcase/Marquee once eligible. |
| 9–12 | Second wave | $8,000 | Second song, or remix/sped-up winner, through same test-and-scale. Retarget watchers to stream/follow. |
| 13–16 | Peak and lock in | $4,000 | Money on top 2 clips only. Collabs and a live session. Push followers to next release. Final reach report. |

**Content volume:**
- **Matt's accounts:** 1–2 short clips/day across TikTok, Reels, Shorts (10–14/week).
- **New ad creatives:** 3–5/week while testing, 1–2/week while scaling.
- **Longer YouTube video:** 1/week (studio session or mix breakdown).
- **Clippers/creators:** make their own volume; Matt supplies clean audio, stems, a hook guide.

## The automation bot

Five steps in a loop: cut clips → caption → post → move ad money to winners → report. Mostly free tools tied together with n8n; post only through official APIs or approved schedulers so accounts don't get flagged. Tool details: [[Free Tool Stack]].

| Step | What it does | Free / open-source | Paid shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Cut clips | Finds the hook, cuts 7–15 s 9:16 clips | FFmpeg, PySceneDetect, librosa | Opus Clip, Vizard |
| 2. Caption | Transcribes lyrics, times them, burns captions | Whisper/WhisperX + FFmpeg | CapCut (manual), Submagic |
| 3. Schedule and post | Same clip to TikTok, Reels, Shorts with per-platform captions | Postiz or Mixpost; official APIs | Buffer, Metricool, Later |
| 4. Test ads and shift budget | Launches 3–5 ads, pauses losers, moves budget to winners daily | TikTok/Meta Ads Manager automated rules; Ads APIs via n8n | Madgicx, Revealbot-style tools |
| 5. Report | Reach, views, CPM, follows, streams into one sheet weekly | Google Sheets + Looker Studio | Chartmetric, Songstats |

**Starting budget rules:** after $20 spent on an ad, pause it if watch time < account average; double daily budget on any ad with CPM < $4; never move > 30% of a platform's budget in one day.

**Build order:** steps 1, 2, 5 first (most hours saved, no account risk) → step 3 via an approved scheduler → step 4 last, once there are two weeks of ad data. TikTok reviews apps before its posting API can publish publicly.

Higgsfield's virality predictor and trending-sound lookup could score clips before they post.

## Assumptions and open questions

Biggest unknown: Matt's budget — it decides the tier.

- [ ] **Budget:** unknown. Plan assumes $25K; at $5K use the lean split and treat 5M as a stretch.
- [ ] **Songs:** assumes 2–3 finished, released tracks with clean audio and some video.
- [ ] **Audience:** assumes mostly US/English targeting.
- [ ] **Genre:** unknown. Hip-hop, pop, dance travel fastest as clips.
- [ ] **Starting point:** assumes small following. A song with momentum → start at scale phase, save 2–4 weeks.
- **Benchmarks:** vendor/agency blog costs; replace with week 3–4 test results.
